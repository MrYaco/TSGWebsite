Why are we doing this?

The Solarpunk Guild exists because change doesn't happen by waiting for someone else to start. We turn ideas into small, doable quests, so that anyone who wants to build a better, greener, more connected world can find a place to begin. Every quest completed is a step from dreaming to acting to changing the world around us.

Our team

We are a team of passionate individuals dedicated to creating a more sustainable and equitable future. However we understand that we cannot do this alone. which is why we created this platform. Our founder saw that many were motivated to change the world but didn't know where to start. To help them, we created this platform to help them find their first step and start their journey. If you want to join us, please reach out to us by clicking the "Contact us" button at the bottom of the page. We would love to hear from you and work together to create a better future.
