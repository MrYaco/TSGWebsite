Why are we doing this?

The Solarpunk Guild exists because change doesn't happen by waiting for someone else to start. We turn ideas into small, doable quests, so that anyone who wants to build a better, greener, more connected world can find a place to begin. Every quest completed is a step from dreaming to acting to changing the world around us.
