What is the Solarpunk Guild?

The Solarpunk Guild is an adventurers' guild brought into real life. We do not slay dragons or play with magic but we are a community that gathers concrete actions such as citizen science, solo actions near you, local group projects and online contributions. All presented as quests that anyone can take on, with trustworthy instructions and even some rewards!

Our goal

We want to help those yearning for a more sustainable, community-driven future by giving them opportunities to make a real difference in the form of quests.
