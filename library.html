The quest board

The quest board gathers every available quest. Use the filters to choose a quest type (citizen science, solo near you, local group or online) and a difficulty, then click a card to see all its details.

Anatomy of a quest

Each quest has a level from F (easiest) to S (hardest), an archetype that tells which class it helps you grow in, a suggested number of participants, milestones to follow and a link to the official instructions. Quests never require money.
